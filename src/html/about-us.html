About Unisas

Technology that fits your business, not the other way around.

Who We Are

Unisas ITBusiness Solutions Private Limited is a software services company built on a simple belief: technology should serve business goals, not complicate them. We're a young company, but our team brings over 75 years of combined industry experience in ERP implementation, integration and consulting.

We work with businesses of every size to turn complex processes into clear, connected systems that deliver measurable results.

What We Do

Unisas delivers a complete suite of ERP-integrated SaaS applications, available on the cloud or on-premise, designed to run your entire business from a single platform.

Whether you're implementing your first ERP system (greenfield) or modernising an existing one (brownfield), we meet you where you are. No matter how complex your business or technology landscape, your transformation can start immediately.

Our services cover the full lifecycle:

  • Consulting

    understanding your processes before recommending a solution

  • Implementation

    deploying ERP systems tailored to how you actually operate

  • Integration

    connecting your existing tools into one reliable source of truth

  • Ongoing support

    helping your system scale as your business grows

Why ERP Matters

In a fast-moving market, keeping up isn't enough. Efficiency, accuracy and adaptability decide who stays ahead. A well-implemented ERP system gives you:

  • One integrated system

    every part of your value chain connected, with no duplicated effort

  • Reliable data

    centralised information that reduces errors and supports confident decisions

  • Real-time visibility

    live insight into performance so you can respond faster

  • Better collaboration

    every department working from the same information

  • Lower costs

    fewer manual tasks and leaner processes over time

  • Room to grow

    a system that scales and adapts as your business expands

  • Built-in compliance

    automated processes and audit trails that support regulatory requirements

  • Happier customers

    faster response times and smoother order fulfilment

  • A fit for your business

    configurable to your specific workflows

  • Stronger security

    centralised data with controlled access
