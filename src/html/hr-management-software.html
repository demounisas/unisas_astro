JOINERS, MOVERS, LEAVERS

How Can Odoo Simplify Employee Onboarding, Changes and Offboarding?

Each joiner, mover and leaver becomes a plan of activities assigned to the right people, and every change is logged on the record. Try all three below.

  • OnboardingKarthik joins the plant

    IT creates his login, his manager books induction and HR collects ID proofs. Every task has an owner and a due date.

  • ChangePriya is promoted

    Her position, manager and department update once. Approvals and the org chart follow, and the history shows what changed.

  • OffboardingSenthil resigns

    Equipment returns, final settlement and access removal run as a checklist. The record is archived, not deleted.
