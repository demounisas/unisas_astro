THE COST OF MANAGING PROJECTS BY HAND

Is Your Project Delivery Process Difficult to Plan, Track or Bill Without a Project Management System?

Most teams still run delivery on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and memory. That holds for two or three jobs. Around ten, the cracks show. Without proper project tracking software, every status question becomes a phone call and every invoice becomes a guess.

  • Plans live in a spreadsheet that is out of date by Tuesday
  • Nobody knows which task is holding up the next one
  • Status meetings exist only to find out where things stand
  • Hours are logged on paper, in a chat, or not at all
  • Invoices go out late because nobody knows what can be billed

Each gap looks small on its own. Together they mean slipped deadlines, unbilled hours and clients who hear about a delay last.

      DATA, ROLES & CUSTOMIZATION

      How Does Unisas Handle Project Data, Roles and Odoo Customization?

      Each person sees what their role needs and nothing more. We configure first, use Odoo Studio for light changes, and write code only when the process truly needs it.

      • Project ManagerProject: Administrator

        Sees every project, the Gantt plan and margins. Creates projects from templates and posts updates.

      • Team MemberProject: User

        Lands on My Tasks. Moves tasks and logs time, but never sees rates or margins.

      • ClientPortal user

        Sees only their project. Approves work or requests changes, without internal notes or costs.

      • AccountsInvoicing: Billing

        Invoices timesheets and milestones, but cannot edit tasks or plans.
