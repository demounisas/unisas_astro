Can Your Inventory Management System Keep Up With Your Growing Operations?

More SKUs, more locations and more sales channels all strain the same weak point: one file that is never quite current. Four people edit it, three tabs disagree, and the formula in C14 broke last week.

  • Stock figures differ between the warehouse, sales and accounts
  • Shortages surface only after the order is promised
  • Each branch keeps its own sheet, updated at day end
  • Nobody can say which batch went to which customer
  • Stock value is worked out once a month, by hand

Two or more sound familiar? Your business has outgrown spreadsheets and standalone billing tools. An inventory management system excel file simply cannot record what happens on the floor as it happens.

    REPLENISHMENT

    How Can Odoo Reordering Rules Automate Replenishment and Stock Movement?

    Running out is rarely a buying problem; it is a timing problem. Each day, Odoo compares forecasted stock against your rules and either alerts you or simply raises the order for items about to run short. This is where inventory replenishment software pays for itself.

    • Reordering rulesSet min and max per product and warehouse, so purchase orders go out on time.
    • Make to orderA confirmed sale can raise its own PO or manufacturing order.
    • Resupply between warehousesBranches top up from your main warehouse automatically.
    • Putaway rulesReceived goods are directed straight to the right shelf.
