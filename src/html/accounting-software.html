WHAT IT SHOULD MANAGE

What Should the Best Accounting Software Actually Help Your Business Manage?

Bookkeeping is only the start. A good accounting system runs the daily finance work and keeps the books current as transactions happen. For an Indian company, it also has to handle GST, e-Invoicing and Tally history without workarounds. Here are the eight areas we set up, and what each one replaces.

  • 01Customer invoicing

    GST invoices with HSN codes, e-Invoice IRN and payment links, created from sales orders or deliveries.

    Replaces Typing invoices in Tally after the sale

  • 02Vendor bills

    Bills read from email, matched to purchase orders and receipts, then approved before payment.

    Replaces Bills checked by hand against challans

  • 03Bank & cash

    Statement lines imported daily and matched to open invoices and bills automatically.

    Replaces Ticking bank statements in Excel

  • 04GST & TDS

    CGST, SGST and IGST computed on every line, with GSTR-1 and 3B figures ready to file. This is accounting software with GST inside the ledger, not added at filing time.

    Replaces Rebuilding GST figures every month

  • 05Receivables & follow-up

    Overdue invoices by age, with reminders sent on a schedule. Accounts receivable aging stays current every morning.

    Replaces Calling customers from a list

  • 06Assets & depreciation

    An asset register with depreciation posted every month.

    Replaces Year-end depreciation worksheet

  • 07Budgets & cost centres

    Budgets per branch or project, compared with actuals through analytic accounts.

    Replaces Branch P&Ls built from exports

  • 08Closing & reports

    Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow, live at any date, with lock dates once filed.

    Replaces Waiting for month end to see numbers

BEFORE GO-LIVE

What Does Your Business Need to Configure Before Going Live With GST Accounting Software?

Nine settings decide whether your books are right from day one. We set each with your accountant.

  1. 01Fiscal localization

    The India accounting package: Indian chart of accounts, GST tax groups and GSTR reports. GSTIN and state set, so CGST + SGST or IGST applies correctly.

  2. 02Chart of accounts

    Tally ledgers mapped to Odoo accounts and types, so the Balance Sheet and P&L group correctly.

  3. 03Taxes

    Current GST slabs, reverse charge and TDS sections such as 194C, with fiscal positions for exports and SEZ.

  4. 04Journals & banks

    One bank journal per account and cash journals per branch.

  5. 05Payment terms & reminders

    30 days, 50% advance or end-of-month terms, plus reminders at 7, 15 and 30 days overdue.

  6. 06Fiscal year & lock dates

    April to March fiscal year, locked after each filed return so nobody posts into a closed period.

  7. 07Opening balances

    Trial balance at cut-over plus every open invoice and bill, so ageing is right from day one.

  8. 08Documents & e-Invoicing

    Invoice layout with HSN and bank details, plus e-Invoice IRN and e-Way Bill credentials on the GST portal.

  9. 09Users & access

    Billing for the billing team, Accountant for finance, read-only Auditor access for your CA.
