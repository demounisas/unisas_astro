Is Your Sales Process Ready for Connected Sales Tracking Software?

Most growing teams don't see the cost of scattered selling until a customer is waiting and nobody has the answer. Prices sit in inboxes, stock is checked after the promise, and the same order is typed three times before the money arrives.

  • Prices and discounts are buried in old emails and spreadsheets
  • Orders are confirmed before anyone checks stock
  • Accounts re-types every order into an invoice
  • Nobody can find the latest signed quote
  • Monthly reports are rebuilt by hand

If two or more sound familiar, your team has outgrown spreadsheets. The fix is one system that holds the quote, the order and the invoice in one record, so every department works from the same numbers.

HOW WE IMPLEMENT

How Can Unisas, an Odoo Implementation Partner, Fit Odoo Around Your Sales Process?

You won't get a blank system and a login. Instead, we study how you sell first, then configure Odoo's standard settings to match. Custom code comes last, and only where a real gap exists.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Odoo Sales Module Implementation

Still have a question?Talk to an Odoo consultant about your sales process.Ask us
How long does an Odoo Sales implementation take?

A focused rollout usually takes four to eight weeks. Connecting Inventory and Accounting, migrating data and building custom documents adds time. Unisas confirms the exact timeline in your fixed-scope proposal before any work begins.

Can we keep our current quotation format?

Yes. We rebuild your quotation, order and invoice layouts inside Odoo with your branding, fields and terms. Customers see familiar documents, while your team gains real quotation software with templates, optional products and validity dates.

Can customers sign and pay online?

Yes. Customers receive a secure link to review the quote, sign it and pay a deposit or the full amount by UPI or card. Odoo e signature confirms the order instantly, so nobody chases paperwork.

Does Odoo Sales handle GST and e-Invoicing?

Yes. Indian GST taxes and the e-Invoice (IRN) integration are part of Odoo's Indian localisation. We configure both with your accountant, so every invoice from your sales management software carries the correct tax details automatically.

Can we import our existing customers, products and prices?

Yes. We clean and import customers, products, variants and pricelists from Excel, Tally or your current system before go-live. Your team starts on familiar data inside the Odoo sales module from day one.

Can Odoo Sales work with our existing CRM or eCommerce site?

Yes. Odoo CRM and Odoo eCommerce connect natively, sharing one customer record. Other platforms, including marketplaces and courier services, link through APIs or connectors, which our team scopes during the Fit-GAP workshop.
