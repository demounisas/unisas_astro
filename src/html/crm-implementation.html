WHAT IS ODOO CRM

What Is Odoo CRM and How Can It Improve Your Sales Process?

Think of it as the Odoo CRM module that runs every deal inside Odoo ERP. It follows every lead from first enquiry to signed deal and shares data with Sales and Invoicing. Most teams we meet run sales across a shared inbox, a spreadsheet and a few phones. A good customer relationship management implementation replaces all three with one screen your whole team trusts. AUTOMATION

What CRM Processes Can Unisas Automate?

We automate the steps between sales stages using Odoo CRM automation rules, so your team spends its day talking to buyers instead of updating fields.

  • TRIGGER · ON CREATION

    WHENA website form or email creates a lead

    THENTag the source and assign the right salesperson by territory

  • TRIGGER · ON SAVE

    WHENA lead matches your ideal-customer rules

    THENSet priority to high and move it up the salesperson's list

  • TRIGGER · BASED ON DATE FIELD

    WHENLast stage update is more than 7 days ago

    THENSchedule a follow-up call and notify the sales manager

  • TRIGGER · STAGE IS SET TO

    WHENStage is set to Proposition

    THENCreate the quotation from your template, ready to send

  • TRIGGER · STAGE IS SET TO

    WHENStage is set to Won

    THENConfirm the sales order and hand over to delivery

  • TRIGGER · ON ARCHIVED

    WHENA deal is marked lost

    THENLog the lost reason and add the contact to a nurture campaign
